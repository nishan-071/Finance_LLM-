Q: Explain in two sentences what a 10-K filing is.
A: A **10-K filing** is an annual report that publicly traded companies in the U.S. must submit to the **Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)**, detailing their financial performance, business operations, risks, and governance over the past year.

It provides comprehensive information for investors, regulators, and stakeholders, including audited financial statements, management discussions, and strategic outlook.
